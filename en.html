Dr. Lucian Zane Cross

Senior Researcher · Department of Anomalous Biological Specimens · Project Lead
Site-19 · Level 3

Profile

Senior Researcher at Site-19, Department of Anomalous Biological Specimens. Leads research teams assigned to containment projects and coordinates testing across them. Covers intake, registration, isolation, culture and inactivation assessment of anomalous biological tissue, and supplies written grounds for containment adjustments within the department. Judgement rests on microscopic structure and cellular behaviour. Doctoral training at MIT in Biological Engineering and Microbiology, covering isolation protocols for unknown-pathogen analogues, structural anomalies at the tissue level, and physical containment of biological specimens.

Current work centres on cryo-preserved tissue; responsible for structural stability review and periodic monitoring under containment, to determine whether specimens drift under existing conditions. Also handles research scheduling and conclusion review for Unit 2, and coordinates testing methods across projects. Full record: Curriculum Vitae.
