Site-19 · Level 3

Profile

Senior Researcher at Site-19, Department of Anomalous Biological Specimens. Leads research teams assigned to containment projects and coordinates testing across them. Covers intake, registration, isolation, culture and inactivation assessment of anomalous biological tissue, and supplies written grounds for containment adjustments within the department. Judgement rests on microscopic structure and cellular behaviour rather than testimony, field accounts or indirect reporting; where experimental data conflicts with narrative material, the data stands. Doctoral training at MIT in Biological Engineering and Microbiology, covering isolation protocols for unknown-pathogen analogues, structural anomalies at the tissue level, and physical containment of biological specimens.

Signed reports are fixed in structure and short in content — number, conclusion, necessary action, nothing further; no extended discussion, no speculative remarks. Also handles periodic review of cryo-preserved specimens and containment stability monitoring. Current work centres on cryo-preserved tissue. Resident at site; no external duties; rarely appears on jointly signed documents. Full record: Curriculum Vitae.
